Welcome.Marhaba.

Sand Gate Camp sits in the small oasis of Abu Monkar, on the western edge of the Libyan Desert — a caravanserai of earth and palm wood, built to rest between the oases and the dunes.

LocationAbu Monkar oasis
BuiltFrom 2009
Plot5,000 m²

Sunset today--:--

A caravanseraifor modern travellers.

Construction began in 2009, using the traditional building methods of the oases. The camp occupies a 5,000 m² plot surrounded by around 80 hectares of palm groves and fruit trees.

Its layout follows the old caravanserais that once sheltered travellers on the desert roads: rooms around shaded courtyards, thick earth walls that keep the heat out, and terraces open to the horizon. Local materials meet a warm, minimal interior.

It is our base for crossing the desert — the place where expeditions rest, regroup and set out again towards the Great Sand Sea.
