Ground servicesin Egypt.

We provide the services directly, so your clients deal with one team from the airport to the dunes.

  • Airport reception and transfers in Cairo and the oases
  • Hotels in Cairo and in the oases
  • Microbus and 4×4 vehicles with Bedouin drivers
  • Our own base: Sand Gate Camp in Abu Monkar
  • Desert camps with tents and full camp kitchen
  • Guides, cameleers and trekking support
  • GPS navigation and satellite communication
  • Visits to Giza, Saqqara and the Grand Egyptian Museum

Routes that adaptto your clients.
